# Sources

Harvester does not invent KMA fish counts. Official numbers shown in the
almanac come from published ADF&G documents. Player catch is a single-permit
simulation and is never presented as district or weekly harvest.

## Area and gear (regulation)

- **5 AAC 18.100** Description of area (Kodiak Management Area / KMA clip).
- **5 AAC 18.200** Fishing districts and sections.
- **5 AAC 18.320** Fishing periods by emergency order.
- **5 AAC 18.330** Gear: set gillnet (S04K) only in the Central Section of the
  Northwest Kodiak District, and in inner Alitak sections (Alitak Bay, Moser
  Bay, Olga Bay, Dog Salmon Flats, Upper Station, Akalura) until 4 September,
  after which seines may also fish those inner sections.
- **5 AAC 18.331** Set gillnet specifications (Central Section 175 fathom
  aggregate).
- **5 AAC 18.361** Alitak District Salmon Management Plan.
- **5 AAC 18.362** Westside Kodiak Salmon Management Plan.
- Booklet: *2024–2027 Kodiak Area Commercial Salmon Fishing Regulations*,
  ADF&G, electronic update 15 July 2025.

## 2025 openings (emergency orders)

Openings in the game calendar are taken from published 2025 Kodiak commercial
salmon advisory announcements / EOs and from the 2025 season summary. Where an
EO PDF was not available here, the season summary’s qualitative description is
used (for example “105-hour weekly fishing periods were allowed in July” and
“extended fishing time was allowed until late August”). Extra week-by-district
openings are **not** invented.

- EO **4-FS-K-01-25**, Advisory #01, 4 June 2025
  (https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/static/applications/dcfnewsrelease/1674400343.pdf)
- EO **4-FS-K-02-25**, Advisory #02, Alitak 33-hour period 9–10 June 2025
  (https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/static/applications/dcfnewsrelease/1675575955.pdf)
- EO **4-FS-K-11-25**, Advisory #11, 105-hour period beginning 6 July 2025
  (https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/static/applications/dcfnewsrelease/1695213839.pdf)
- *2025 Kodiak Commercial Salmon Season Summary*, 29 October 2025
  (https://www.adfg.alaska.gov/static/applications/dcfnewsrelease/1744070930.pdf)

There is **no 2025 Annual Management Report** in this tree. Do not treat
player or simulated permit catch as official week-by-district harvest.

## 2025 run strength and area totals

From the 29 October 2025 season summary (commercial harvest, excluding
personal use and ADF&G test fish):

| Species | 2025 harvest | 2015–2024 avg | 2025 forecast |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Chinook | 1,315 | 7,558 | 8,300 |
| Sockeye | 1,436,881 | 2,300,090 | 1,575,800 |
| Coho | 315,681 | 296,695 | 203,000 |
| Pink | 34,605,698 | 19,726,543 | 31,800,000 |
| Chum | 740,485 | 673,668 | 615,000 |
| **Total** | **37,100,060** | **23,004,553** | **34,202,100** |

Effort: 142 purse seine and 105 set gillnet permits fished; fewer than three
beach seine permits (confidential). Estimated common-property exvessel value
about $36.9 million. Average earnings: purse seine $241,807 / fished permit;
set gillnet $23,677 / fished permit.

Qualitative run notes used for permit-scale catch *modifiers* (not district
quotas): Karluk early sockeye very weak; westside closed 1 June–5 July for
Chinook conservation; Ayakulik early sockeye average and BEG exceeded; Frazer
weak with Alitak closed late June and most of July; pinks second-largest on
record, especially Northwest and Eastside; Northwest chums particularly
strong; Chinook lowest in 43 years (except 1989).

Preliminary 2025 Kodiak exvessel prices and average weights (ADF&G
*2025 Preliminary Alaska Commercial Salmon Harvests and Exvessel Values*,
updated 6 October 2025): Chinook 5.3 lb / $0.10; sockeye 4.8 lb / $1.34;
coho 7.1 lb / $0.70; pink 3.2 lb / $0.30; chum 6.5 lb / $0.39.

## Weather

- **PADQ** — Kodiak Airport METAR/ASOS hourly, 2025, via Iowa Environmental
  Mesonet ASOS request.
- **NDBC 46077** — Shelikof Strait buoy standard meteorological observations,
  2025, NOAA NDBC THREDDS (`46077h2025.nc`).
- **CWFAER / PKZ** — NWS Alaska coastal waters zones PKZ132 (Kodiak Island
  waters) and PKZ138 (Shelikof Strait). Headlines in-game are derived from
  that day’s 46077 wind and seas, not invented zone text.
